AI Clinical Triage Protocol

Clinical Safety Protocol
🟢 Routine Tier

GREEN Protocol

Criteria: Chronic conditions, mild cough/cold, general follow-ups, minor joint aches.

  • Directly displays top-rated doctors sorted by specialty and hospital distance.
  • Suggests self-care hydration and standard clinic appointment slots.
🟡 Urgent Tier

AMBER Protocol

Criteria: High fever (>102°F), acute abdominal pain, suspected fractures, severe ear infections.

  • Promotes Same-Day Priority Slots within the next 4 hours.
  • Pre-fills clinical urgency summary for the attending doctor's queue.
🔴 Emergency Stop

RED Protocol

Criteria: Radiating chest pain, sudden slurred speech, unconsciousness, severe hemorrhaging.

  • Immediately halts booking wizard.
  • Flashes emergency alert & renders 1-click Call 112 button.
  • Displays nearest 24/7 trauma centers & ICU facilities.

Targeted Multi-Turn Clarification: When patient complaints overlap (e.g., "headache with blurry vision"), the AI does not guess—it triggers one precise clinical question ("Did the vision change happen suddenly, or have you had fever for days?") to distinguish Neurology from Ophthalmology.

Core Feature 2

Zero Double-Booking Concurrency Engine

Dual-Layer Concurrency Architecture

Dual-Layer Concurrency Defense

1
Layer 1: Distributed Redis Mutex (`SET NX EX 180`)

When a user clicks a slot, it is locked immediately for 180 seconds. A real-time countdown hold prevents any other user from selecting it during review.

2
Layer 2: Database Unique Composite Constraint

At commit time, the schema enforces @@unique([doctorId, startTime]). Any concurrent collision is rejected at the database engine level with zero data corruption.

Compliance & Governance

Security & India DPDP Act 2023 Compliance

Privacy by Design
🔐

AES-256 Data Encryption

  • Clinical consultation notes, symptom logs, and patient records are encrypted at rest using industry-standard AES-256-GCM.
  • Cryptographic keys stored securely via environment variables with zero plaintext database exposure.
🛡️

Zero PII to AI Models

  • Patient PII (names, phone numbers, email addresses, IP addresses) is strictly sanitized prior to clinical triage calls.
  • AI evaluation processes only sanitized symptom text and duration metadata, preventing unauthorized data mining.
📜

DPDP Act 2023 Principles

  • Informed Consent: Explicit consent banner before symptom submission.
  • Purpose Limitation: Data used strictly for appointment scheduling.
  • Right to Erasure ("Delete My Data"): 1-click button in profile permanently purging all personal records from the DB.
🗣️ Presenter Note: Emphasize that healthcare applications in India must adhere to the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act of 2023. MediBook AI satisfies this by implementing data minimization, anonymization, and the Right to Erasure.
